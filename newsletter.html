EVERY THURSDAY

The Thursday dispatch

One email a week out of the Portland test kitchen. Three recipes that cleared three rounds, one tool worth the money, what is cheap at the market, and one thing that failed on the bench with the reason it failed.

What actually arrives

The dispatch sends Thursday morning, US Pacific Time, ahead of the weekend shop. Four fixed sections, in the same order every week:

  • Three recipes. One fast weeknight dinner, one that earns a weekend, one bake. Every one of them has cleared three rounds of testing, with the last round cooked by somebody who did not write it.
  • One ingredient or tool we have actually been using, with what it costs and whether it earned the counter space. If the link is an affiliate link, the line above it says so.
  • What is worth buying this week, tracked against the US growing calendar and, in season, what is turning up at the Portland markets.
  • One failure. Something that went wrong on the bench, the variable that caused it and what we changed. This section gets more replies than the other three combined.

That is the entire format. It has not moved since 2021 and we have no plans to move it.

What does not arrive

  • Daily email. One send a week, on Thursday.
  • Mail from anybody else. We do not rent, sell or share the list.
  • Unlabeled advertising. At most one sponsor slot per issue, marked as a sponsor slot.
  • Anything you cannot leave. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe at the bottom.
  • A recipe we have not cooked three times. There is no filler section.

How we handle your email, in full

Who is sending it. Saltgrain Kitchen Co., 2219 NE Alberta Street, Suite 4, Portland, OR 97211, United States. That physical mailing address appears in the footer of every email we send, as the CAN-SPAM Act requires.

How to leave. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer. It works immediately and we process removals promptly. You can also email privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com and we will remove you by hand.

Confirmed opt-in. After you submit this form we send exactly one confirmation email. You are not on the list until you click the link in it, so a mistyped address never receives mail and nobody can sign up an address that is not theirs.

What we store. Your email address, your first name if you gave one, your topic preference, and the date and time you subscribed. We keep the consent record because we have to be able to show consent was given.

What we never do. Sell, rent or share your address; add you to a list you did not ask for; send a sponsor's email on their behalf; or use a misleading subject line or a false header.

Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Why we ask you to confirm

Confirmed opt-in, sometimes called double opt-in, costs you one extra click and gives us a list where every address asked to be there. It catches typos, it stops anyone signing up an address that is not theirs, and it keeps the list honest when we quote subscriber numbers in the media kit.

The trade-off is that the confirmation email sometimes lands in a spam folder. If nothing has arrived in ten minutes, look there, then add our sending address to your contacts so the weekly issue lands where you expect it.

We do not use the newsletter to sell you anything beyond the labeled sponsor slot and the occasional mention of our own downloads. If that ever changes, you will read about it in an issue before it happens, not after.

What we measure, and what we do with it

We track four numbers per issue: delivery rate, open rate, click rate by link position, and unsubscribes. They go in the media kit and they decide what gets written next. If a recipe in the dispatch draws a high click rate and then generates reader reports of the same failure, that recipe goes back on the bench and the correction runs in the following issue rather than quietly on the page. We do not track what you do after you leave the email, we do not build a profile from your reading, and we do not pass any of these figures to a sponsor with your address attached to them. Aggregate numbers only.
