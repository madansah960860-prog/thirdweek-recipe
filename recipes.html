How to read a recipe here

Every recipe page carries the same information in the same order, so you can find the one number you came for without reading the page top to bottom.

  • Times and yield sit directly under the photograph: prep, cook, total, servings, difficulty and course.
  • Allergen tags list which of the nine major US allergens appear in the ingredient list as written. Substitutions are covered further down.
  • The measurement toggle switches every quantity on the page between US customary and metric. A US cup is 240 ml and a US tablespoon is 15 ml here. Gram weights were read off a scale, not calculated from a cup.
  • The servings scaler recalculates each quantity live. Pan diameter and cooking time do not scale linearly, so we flag every place where that matters.
  • Ingredient checklists are grouped by component and can be ticked as you shop or cook. Ticks survive a page refresh.
  • Storage, reheating and make-ahead notes appear after the method, with food safety guidance sourced to the USDA and reheating temperatures given in °F and °C.
  • Nutrition estimates, where we include them, sit at the very end and are clearly labeled as estimates.

More on the conventions we use is on the kitchen basics page.

What tested means on this site

A recipe goes up after at least three full cooks. The last round is run by someone who did not develop it, working only from the published text. We record pan diameter, oven position, internal temperature and wall-clock time on every pass, then publish those figures rather than a rounded guess. When two testers get different results, we cook it again until we can explain the difference.

  • Weights are measured, not converted. Gram figures come off a scale. We do not multiply a cup by an assumed density.
  • Times are wall-clock. Total time starts when you walk into the kitchen, with the chopping counted, not after everything is already prepped.
  • Failure modes are named. If a step broke for us in testing, the warning is written into the recipe instead of left out of it.
  • Substitutions are tested too. A swap appears in a recipe only if we cooked that version as well.
