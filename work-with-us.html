PARTNERSHIPS

Work with the test kitchen

Advertising, sponsored recipes, newsletter slots and longer partnerships. Rates in USD, the editorial rules that apply to all of them, and a plain list of what we decline.

We take on a small number of brands each year. The reason to be selective is commercial, not sentimental: what makes this site worth buying is that readers act on what it says, and a placement that erodes that is worth less than the fee attached to it.

A sponsored recipe here goes through the same three rounds of testing as an unsponsored one, and is cooked in round three by somebody who did not develop it. That is the product you are buying. It is also why turnaround is measured in weeks rather than days.

Everything below is public on purpose. If you are a brand, you know exactly what you are getting and what you are not. If you are a reader, you know what a sponsorship label means and what a sponsor can and cannot influence.

What we offer

Display advertising

Standard IAB units in fixed slots on recipe, category and article pages. Slots sit between content sections and in the sidebar. Never above the recipe, never adjacent to navigation or buttons, never on a legal page, and never expanding over a method step.

Sold programmatically through our ad partner. For a direct buy, use the form below.

Sponsored recipe

from $2,400per recipe, USD

An original recipe developed and tested here using your product, photographed here, published on a page that carries a sponsorship label above the headline.

Three rounds of testing, three rounds of photography, the full recipe page with our standard test notes, one newsletter mention and social posts. Typical turnaround is four weeks, and the bottleneck is the testing, not the writing.

Newsletter sponsorship

from $650per issue, USD

One labeled sponsor slot per issue: around 60 words, one image and one link. Everything else in the email is editorial and written by us. We do not run two sponsors in one send.

Media kit with audience figures, open rates and click rates available on request through the form below.

Brand partnership

customquoted per project, USD

Longer work: a series of recipes, a seasonal collection built around Pacific Northwest produce, or a downloadable guide produced with a brand. Minimum three months, because a multi-recipe series needs a testing schedule rather than a deadline.

Every page in a partnership carries the same top-of-page label as a single sponsored recipe. No exceptions for volume.

The rules, and they are not negotiable

  • Every paid placement is labeled, at the top. A sponsored recipe carries a sponsorship label above the headline, before the first line of copy. Newsletter slots are marked as sponsor slots. This meets FTC guidance on endorsements under 16 CFR Part 255, and we would do it anyway.
  • The testing standard does not change for a fee. A sponsored recipe clears three rounds, with round three cooked by somebody who did not write it, exactly like everything else here. If it does not clear, it does not publish.
  • We write the copy. Sponsors may check factual claims about their own product. They do not approve, edit or veto our editorial copy, our method, our temperatures or our conclusions.
  • We report what the tests showed. If a product underperforms in the method, we say so and we tell you before publication. If we cannot produce something that passes on its own merits, we do not publish and we return the fee. We have done that twice.
  • No paid positive verdicts. We do not sell conclusions. If you need a guaranteed favourable result, we are the wrong kitchen.
  • No health, medical or weight claims. We will not describe a product as healthy, clean, detoxifying or fat-burning, and we will not frame a recipe around calorie restriction. This is a firm limit and it has cost us work.
  • No competitor comparisons unless we have tested both under the same conditions, and then only in an unsponsored piece.
  • No link insertions into published work. We do not sell links in existing editorial and we do not accept paid guest posts.
  • Categories we decline: supplements, weight-loss products, meal-replacement programs, alcohol marketed to young audiences, and anything making a health claim we cannot substantiate.

Media kit

The media kit carries monthly page views, unique readers, newsletter subscriber count, open and click rates, audience geography and age split, top-performing content, and full technical specifications for every ad unit and sponsored format.

We do not print audience figures on a public page, because they move every month and a stale number is worse than no number. Request the current kit through the form below and the latest PDF goes out the same week, usually within two business days.

In summary: the audience is overwhelmingly United States based, skews toward people who cook dinner at home several times a week, and reads on mobile more than desktop. Traffic peaks Sunday evening and Thursday morning, which is why the newsletter sends on Thursday.

What we will ask you for

Before we quote a sponsored recipe we need four things from you: the product in the exact retail format a reader would buy, a written list of the claims you want checked, the date you need the page live, and the name of the one person who signs off on factual accuracy. We do not need creative direction and we will not take a script. Expect us to ask whether the product is stocked nationally, because we will not build a weeknight recipe around something most readers cannot find on a shelf.

Get in touch

Business enquiries are kept for 36 months for accounting purposes and then deleted. They are not added to the newsletter list.

What a sponsorship label looks like

This is the exact component that sits at the top of any sponsored recipe page, above the headline:

Sponsored recipe

Directly below it goes one line naming the sponsor, stating that they paid for the recipe to be developed, and stating that the method, the temperatures and the verdict were not reviewed by them.

There are no sponsored recipes live on the site at present. When there are, they will look exactly like this and never anything quieter.
