Cookie policy

Every category of cookie and storage key we set, what each one does, how long it lasts, and how to change your mind in two clicks.

Last updated 09/14/2026. This page is written in plain English wherever we can manage it. If anything here is unclear, ask us and we will explain it and probably rewrite it.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. It lets a site remember something between page loads, such as which measurement units you chose. Similar technologies include local storage, which keeps data on your device without sending it back to us, and pixels, which are tiny images used to record that something loaded.

This policy uses the word cookie to cover all of them.

Your choices, and how to change them

On your first visit a banner appears at the bottom of the page with three options: accept all, reject non-essential, or manage preferences. Until you choose, only strictly necessary cookies are set. No analytics, no advertising.

You can change your decision at any time using the Cookie Settings link, which appears in the footer of every page on this site. Your choice is stored on your own device, so if you use several browsers or devices you will be asked on each of them.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working properly.

What this means in practice

Three storage keys keep this site usable: your cookie choice, your units toggle and your ticked ingredient boxes. The last two never leave your browser. Nothing in the analytics or advertising tables below is set until you press Accept all or tick the box yourself. Choose Reject non-essential and you still get the whole site, every recipe and every conversion chart, with generic advertising in place of selected advertising. Change your mind at any point with the Cookie Settings link in the footer, and note that clearing your browser storage resets the whole lot.

The categories we use

Strictly necessary

These make the site work and cannot be switched off. They do not track you across other sites and they are not used for advertising.

The last three are browser local storage rather than cookies. They stay on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Analytics, set only with your consent

These tell us which pages are used and how people arrive, in aggregate. We use this to decide what to test and write next. They are not used to identify you personally and the data is not sold.

Advertising, set only with your consent

Advertising pays for this site. With your consent, our advertising partners set cookies that help select which ads to show and measure whether they worked.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of many other vendors at aboutads.info/choices or optout.networkadvertising.org.

If you reject advertising cookies you will still see advertisements, because they are what keeps the recipes free. They will simply be generic rather than selected based on your browsing.

What we do not do

  • We do not set non-essential cookies before you consent.
  • We do not use cookie walls. Rejecting cookies gives you the whole site.
  • We do not use fingerprinting to get around your choice.
  • We do not sell the contents of your cookies to data brokers.

Do Not Sell or Share

Under California law, sharing identifiers with advertising partners for personalised advertising may count as a sale or share. The Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link in the footer turns advertising cookies off on this device. See the privacy policy for your full rights.

Browser-level controls

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies, usually under Settings, then Privacy. Some browsers offer a Global Privacy Control signal; we treat that signal as a valid opt-out of advertising cookies.

Bear in mind that clearing your cookies also clears your recorded choice here, so you will see the banner again, and it clears your saved ingredient ticks.

Changes

If we add a cookie category or change a vendor we will update this page and the date at the top, and the consent banner will reappear so you can make a fresh choice.
