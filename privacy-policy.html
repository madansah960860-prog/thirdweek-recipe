Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who else touches it, how long we keep it, and how to make it stop. Cookies, Google advertising cookies, CCPA and CPRA rights, and a GDPR note.

Last updated 09/14/2026. This page is written in plain English wherever we can manage it. If anything here is unclear, ask us and we will explain it and probably rewrite it.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information Saltgrain Kitchen Co. collects when you use saltgrainkitchen.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. It applies to this website and to our email newsletter.

The data controller is Saltgrain Kitchen Co., 2219 NE Alberta Street, Suite 4, Portland, OR 97211, United States. That address is the test kitchen, the office and the postal route for every request described on this page.

What this means in practice

The short version, in plain English. We collect an email address only if you hand us one, plus the standard server logs any website keeps. Analytics and advertising cookies stay switched off until you switch them on, and the Cookie Settings link in the footer switches them back off on the spot. Your measurement preference and your ticked ingredient boxes stay in your own browser and never reach us. If you want your record deleted, one email to privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com does it, inside the deadlines set out below. The rest of this page is the precise version, and the precise version is the one that binds us.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signup: your email address, your first name if you provide it, your topic preference if you choose one, and the date, time and IP address of your consent.
  • Contact and enquiry forms: your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, the subject you select and the message you write.
  • Purchases: if you buy a download or a membership, our payment processor collects your billing details. We never see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and timestamps. These are collected for security and to keep the site running.
  • Analytics, only with your consent: pages visited, time on page, approximate location at city level, device type and how you arrived at the site.
  • Advertising, only with your consent: identifiers used by advertising partners to select and measure ads.
  • Local storage on your own device: your measurement preference, your ticked ingredient boxes and your cookie choice. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for and do not want: government identification numbers, health information, financial account details, precise geolocation, or any special category data. Please do not send us any of that through a contact form.

How we use your information

  • To send the newsletter you asked for.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To deliver purchases and provide membership access.
  • To understand which recipes are used, so we know what to test next. Aggregated, with consent.
  • To display advertising, which pays for the site. With consent, some of it is personalised.
  • To keep the site secure and to investigate abuse.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies. On your first visit you are asked to choose between accepting all, rejecting everything that is not essential, or managing your preferences individually. No analytics or advertising cookies are set before you make that choice.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page. The cookie policy lists every category and what each one does.

Third parties, including Google

We share personal information only with service providers who need it to do a job for us, and only for that job.

Advertising partners

We work with third-party advertising vendors, which may include Google, to display ads on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices, or through the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • You can also decline advertising cookies entirely through our own cookie banner, in which case you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Other processors

  • Web hosting and content delivery: serves the pages and keeps server logs.
  • Email service provider: stores the newsletter list and sends the emails.
  • Payment processor: handles purchases of downloads and memberships.
  • Analytics provider: aggregated usage statistics, only with your consent.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share limited identifiers with advertising partners when you have consented to advertising cookies, which some US state laws define as a sale or a share. See the section below on your California rights.

How long we keep it

  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so we do not accidentally email you again.
  • Contact form messages: 24 months, then deleted.
  • Business and partnership enquiries: 36 months, for accounting purposes.
  • Purchase records: seven years, because tax law requires it.
  • Server logs: 90 days.
  • Analytics data: 26 months in aggregated form.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA and CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it and who we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as the statute defines it.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights. We will not degrade the site or charge you more.

To opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, use the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link, which also appears in the footer of every page on this site. Selecting it turns off advertising cookies immediately on this device. You can also email privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com.

We honor Global Privacy Control browser signals as a valid opt-out request where your browser sends one.

To make any other request, email privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com or use the contact form and choose the privacy subject. We respond within 45 days. We may ask you to verify your identity, usually by confirming the email address associated with your record. An authorised agent may make a request on your behalf with written permission.

Other US state privacy rights

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and others as they take effect, have broadly similar rights to access, correct, delete and opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same contact route above and tell us which state you live in. Where a state provides a right to appeal a refused request, we will explain how to appeal at the point of the refusal.

Note for visitors from the EU, EEA and UK

This site is aimed at readers in the United States, but we are glad to have you. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation applies to our processing of your personal data.

  • Our legal bases: consent for the newsletter and for non-essential cookies; legitimate interests for site security and basic server logs; contract for delivering purchases; legal obligation for accounting records.
  • Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.
  • International transfers: our servers and service providers are located in the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or UK we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved mechanism.
  • Complaints: you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority, or with the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. You must be 13 or older to subscribe to our newsletter, submit a form or create an account.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com and we will delete it promptly. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us about anything a child may have submitted.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to our systems is limited to the three people who run the site, protected by strong unique passwords and two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise, but we do not hold much: we deliberately collect as little personal information as we can get away with.

If you discover a security problem, please email privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com before disclosing it publicly.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, including retailers, recipe sources and government food safety resources. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and their policies apply once you leave here.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, for example a new category of data or a new purpose, we will say so in the newsletter and on the site rather than changing it quietly.

How to contact us about your data

Email privacy@saltgrainkitchen.com, use the contact form and choose the privacy subject, or write to Saltgrain Kitchen Co., 2219 NE Alberta Street, Suite 4, Portland, OR 97211, United States. We aim to answer within five business days and always within the statutory deadline.
